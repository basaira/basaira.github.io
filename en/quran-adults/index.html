Why this matters

01

General feedback does not always tell you whether the issue is makhraj, sifat, timing, tafkhim/tarqiq, or another recitation habit.

Method

Precise correction first. Then deliberate progress.

Basair’s Quran path is designed around oral transmission, direct correction, cumulative memorization and structured review rather than quantity alone.

1

Diagnose

Identify the errors and habits that most affect clarity and correctness.

2

Model & imitate

Hear the correct sound and reproduce it with direct teacher feedback.

3

Stabilize

Repeat corrections until the right pronunciation and Tajweed pattern become more consistent.

4

Advance deliberately

Add memorization or reading targets without abandoning review and previous correction priorities.

FAQ

Before you begin

Do I need to be a beginner?

No. The assessment exists precisely so the path can start from your current level rather than assuming everyone is a beginner.

Can I focus only on Tajweed and correction?

Yes. The track can be shaped around recitation/Tajweed correction without forcing a memorization target that does not match your goal.

Can review be part of the plan?

Yes. Structured near and long-term review is part of the academic methodology when memorization is involved.

Do I create an account first?

No. Requesting an assessment is part of the marketing/admissions journey and does not require a student login.

You should know what you are correcting — and why.
