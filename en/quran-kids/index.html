Recitation errors become habits

Small articulation and Tajweed errors can settle in when they are not corrected precisely and consistently.

02

Memorization without a review system

Method

A Quran learning system built around correction and measurable progress.

The academic method already used at Basair becomes the engine behind the lesson — not a document the parent has to read before booking.

1

Listen & diagnose

The teacher hears the child directly and identifies recitation, Tajweed, articulation, memorization, and review needs.

2

Correct precisely

Correction focuses on sound, makhraj, sifat, tongue/lip/jaw position where needed, and accurate imitation.

3

Build retention

New memorization is paired with cumulative near and long-term review instead of isolated page-by-page progress.

4

Track what changed

The parent can follow the lesson focus, mistakes, progress, notes, and the next objective.

FAQ

Questions families usually ask before starting

Is the assessment really before account creation?

Yes. The acquisition journey is separate from the student product journey. You do not need to create a student account just to request an assessment.

Is this only for memorization?

No. The program can focus on reading, Tajweed, articulation, memorization, review, or a combination depending on the learner’s level.

How do parents know what is improving?

The learning system is designed around corrections, progress, notes and next goals rather than treating the session as an isolated video call.

Can the schedule fit families outside Egypt?

The form collects your country and preferred time/timezone so the admissions team can discuss suitable options with you.
